# Regaarder Studio — AI Director & Creative Suite Vision Roadmap

> Saved from product brainstorming session (2026-11-25) so all requirements persist across chat resets.

## Priority-Ordered Feature Roadmap

### 1. AI Video & Template Creation via Natural Chat (Intro / Body / Outro) — *Priority 1 (Foundational)*
- Through natural chat, the AI can create a complete multi-scene video **or** generate a brand-new reusable template.
- Every generated video/template is structured into **Intro**, **Body**, and **Outro** narrative sections.
- The AI can **borrow and remix** scenes, animations, layouts, and effects from multiple existing templates in the library rather than being locked to a single preset.

### 2. Comment-to-Animate & Auto-Asset Creation — *Priority 2*
- While designing on the stage or storyboard, users can pin comments and natural-language instructions directly onto any scene or item.
- The AI reads those comments, **generates any missing visual assets** (illustrations, cutouts, overlays, backgrounds), and **automatically animates** the item/scene to match.

### 3. Zoomable Storyboard View in Preview — *Priority 3*
- Add a **Storyboard view mode** inside the preview workspace with smooth **zoom in / zoom out** controls.
- Zoom out to inspect the entire **Intro → Body → Outro** flow at once, or zoom in to inspect individual frames, transitions, and pinned comments.

### 4. Click-to-Act Contextual Dropdown (Stage & Storyboard) — *Priority 4*
- Clicking any item on the preview stage **or** in the storyboard opens a unified dropdown menu with **every valid action** for that item type.
- Example (Image): *Set as Background*, *Set as Foreground*, *Cutout / Remove Background*, *Apply Effects*, *Animate*, *Leave AI Comment*, etc.

### 5. Object Cutouts via Natural Chat — *Priority 5*
- Users can ask the AI in natural chat to **cut out or isolate specific objects/subjects** from an image or scene (e.g., *"cut out the speaker"* or *"isolate the product on the right"*) and immediately use them as foreground or 3D layers.

### 6. Granular Text Animation & Style Controls — *Priority 6*
- Dedicated inspector controls for text layers: **animation style** (kinetic, typewriter, reveal, pop, etc.), **animation speed**, **color**, **size**, and typography styling.

### 7. AI Sketch → 3D Render → Texturing, Holes & Sculpting — *Priority 7*
- AI draws/sketches an object concept, converts and renders it into a **3D mesh**, and supports **applying textures**, **cutting/carving holes**, and **sculpting** (push/pull/deform) the 3D geometry.
