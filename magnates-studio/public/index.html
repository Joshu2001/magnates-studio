Document Focus & Highlight Guide

Choose the focus effect that matches your document style, then follow the 2 easy steps:

⚪ Whiteout Focus Light

Best for: Articles, PDFs, tweets. Whites out surrounding text.

⚫ Dark Spotlight Dark

Best for: Dark docs, pitch decks. Dims surroundings into theater mode.

⬛ Product Spotlight Animated

Best for: Products, CTAs, price tags, key stats. Glowing pulsing border with optional label.

How to use in 4 easy steps:
1 Choose a Focus Style above (Whiteout, Dark Spotlight, or Product Spotlight) — each activates the corresponding marquee drag tool on the canvas.
2 Drag Focus Box: Click & drag a rectangle over the area you want to isolate. For Whiteout/Dark, the rest fades. For Product Spotlight, a glowing pulsing border appears.
3 Add Animated Highlights: Click "+ Highlight" and drag across sentences or words to add a live animated marker sweep — each line draws in sequence.
4 Play & Adjust: Hit Spacebar. Tweak fade level, sweep speed, or marker/spotlight color in the Sidebar Inspector anytime.
