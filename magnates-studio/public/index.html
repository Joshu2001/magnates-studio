Article Focus & Document Highlighter Guide

Whiteout Fade & Highlight Articles & News

Best for light articles/screenshots: Fades the surrounding text to light gray/white, keeping the target section razor sharp, followed by animated highlighter sweeps.

Dark Document Spotlight Theater Mode

Best for dark UI / screenshots: Dims the surroundings into theater darkness with a soft vignette, isolating the key paragraph in light.

3-Step Quick Guide

1
Draw Focus Box: Click either button above, then drag a rectangle over the paragraph you want to isolate. The rest of the document will fade back automatically.
2
Add Animated Highlighter: Select Article Highlighter from the toolbar or Inspector, then drag across words/sentences. Each line will dynamically sweep in real time as the playhead advances.
3
Tune & Play: Adjust the Fade Level, Edge Softness, and Sweep Speed anytime in the Sidebar Inspector on the right!
