# Luckin Coffee — Part 2 storyboard (30–60s)

A 28-state continuation to the existing 0–30 second gallery template. This is a storyboard and motion specification, not a finished animation and not a pack of production evidence assets.

## Preview in Magnates Studio

Open the **Luckin Coffee · The Fraud Unravels (30–60s)** card in the Business Storytelling template gallery. The preview is embedded in the Studio flow and lets you scrub the 28 frames, read the voiceover draft, inspect motion choreography, and see what assets will be needed in the next production pass.

## Timing and editing

- 30.0 seconds, 24 fps, 1280×720
- 720 local frames, frames 0–719; full-film frame range 720–1439
- 28 storyboard states with explicit entry, hold, exit, transition, camera, and asset requirements
- `timeline.json` is the source of truth for exact timings and wording
- `index.html` and `renderer.js` render the storyboard preview only; no final animation is produced

## Story progression

The segment moves from the prior open question to SEC-attributed allegations and amounts, the described purchasing schemes and record concealment, discovery during the annual external audit, company response, settlement, and July 2020 delisting. It returns to the coffee business only to pose the next question; it does not claim a specific recovery result.

## Accuracy and asset boundary

The SEC complaint and SEC press release are linked inside `timeline.json`. Every record surface and mechanism diagram is schematic. No receipts, customer orders, auditor marks, company records, named counterparties, or historical footage are fabricated. The amount and legal qualifier remain attributed on screen. The next segment needs primary sources for the date and terms of public disclosure, exact delisting date, and the post-delisting operating business.

The user will request production assets individually. Until then, the stage drawings are storyboard placeholders and the frame notes define how each future layer should move.
