## Ask for a shot only when continuous physical motion matters

Request a new generated or sourced video when a scene depends on motion a still layer cannot reproduce convincingly: a hand manipulating an object, a person walking or turning, a vehicle moving, a camera traveling through a real environment, or a crowd whose motion carries the beat. Do not request a video just to add atmosphere, zoom a still, reveal a document, or animate a graphic; those belong on template tracks.

When a shot is needed, specify all of the following in the request:

1. **Storyboard beat and timeline interval**, plus the exact narration phrase it supports.
2. **Action**, including the start pose, ordered movements, end pose, contact points, and which hand/object moves.
3. **Camera**, including framing, angle, lens feel, camera movement, and whether the camera must stay locked for compositing.
4. **Duration and format**: target length, 16:9, 24 fps, resolution, and whether a clean handle is needed at each end.
5. **Continuity**: same cup, sleeve color, light direction, counter, background, and screen direction as the adjacent plates.
6. **Layering needs**: clean plate, transparent background if supported, empty space for overlays, and no generated text, logos, extra fingers, duplicate objects, or unexplained cuts.
7. **Reference image(s)** and the precise elements that must match. State which details are flexible.
8. **Deliverable check**: ask for a contact sheet at 0.25-second intervals before accepting the clip; verify natural contact, object continuity, shadow/reflection, and clean first/last frames.
