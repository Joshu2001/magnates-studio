# Barry Sacramento · Searchlight Reveal

A separate eight-second Magnates Studio scene template at `/assets/barry-sacramento/`. It competes with, and does not replace, `barry-minkow-opening`.

## Visual choreography

- A single warm flashlight bloom and low boom announce the shot.
- The supplied Sacramento sunset/storm plate appears, then the unfinished tower cutout resolves in front.
- The isolated floodlight rig anchors an editable beam cone that pivots across the tower. Its hot spot and warm reflected streak travel together and are clipped to the building silhouette; the original combined beam plate remains preserved as a source.
- A dense rolling cloud bank drifts along the lower frame behind the title, giving the lettering a clear silhouette while keeping the mist visible beneath it.
- The large, weathered and subtly translucent gold `SACRAMENTO` title holds on a fixed baseline above the cloud bank after the opening beats. Each letter blooms from a small round flare in left-to-right order, then the full horizontal title holds.

The flash-core bloom follows the soft screen-blend optical treatment in `circular-spin-flashlight-glitch`; the unrelated spin, repeated paparazzi strobes, and glitch are not applied.

## Editable controls

Content controls expose the title and visibility of each scene layer. Motion controls expose title timing, searchlight sweep rate, foreground-cloud drift speed, background-sky drift, camera push, and the cloud veil’s vertical offset. Advanced controls expose building and cloud in/out timing plus the building reveal duration. Look controls expose title scale, baseline, cloud offset, title translucency, gold weathering, title gold, and beam tint. Effects controls expose opening bloom, moving reflected-light strength, cloud-bank opacity, and the synthesized opening audio. Motion controls expose the beam pivot range and width. The JSON manifest lists the layer/source mapping and sequence beats. The light uses a soft amber beam haze, restrained warm core and synced orange façade reflection rather than a hard white stripe.

## Run

Open `/assets/barry-sacramento/` from the Magnates Studio server. The page is a self-contained HTML Canvas preview with play, pause, restart, and scrubbing. All preview plates remain separate files in `assets/`.

The building and sky are illustrative generated/user-provided plates, not archival photography or verified documentation.
