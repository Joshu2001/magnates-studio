# Barry Sacramento Interior Fly-Through · Layer Plan

The scene is intentionally split before animation.

1. **Deep corridor plate** — existing clean `construction-corridor.webp`; lowest parallax speed.
2. **Mid architecture** — corridor plate re-drawn through side clips; medium parallax speed.
3. **Walking shadows** — transparent extracted floor-shadow layer; follows people with slightly lower depth.
4. **Two walking figures** — transparent extracted full-frame cutout; independent scale/position.
5. **Near foreground occluders** — left wall, right scaffold/column and ceiling strips clipped from the corridor plate; fastest parallax.
6. **Atmosphere** — procedural amber haze/dust; depth-scaled.
7. **Editable title** — `AN 18-STORY` + `OFFICE BUILDING`; appears only after the camera glide settles.

## Camera choreography

0.00–2.90s: continuous Z push plus downward glide. No orbiting. Foreground planes accelerate past the viewer while the distant corridor moves slowly.

2.90–3.05s: short settle/overshoot.

3.05–4.10s: title resolves in two staggered lines.

4.10–6.50s: composition holds with subtle breathing camera and atmospheric motion.
