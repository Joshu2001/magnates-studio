# Rolls-Royce — opening visual storyboard | 00:00–02:00

**60 atlas windows · 30 fps · 1920×1080 target · 3,600 frames**

This is a template specification reconstructed from the supplied *Rolls-Royce All Frames Visual Atlas*. Each window maps to one atlas page and its 60 source frames. The PDF’s zero-based frame convention is preserved: frame index ÷ 30 = timestamp. This is a reference-led visual grammar and an editable production plan, not a finished animation or a substitute for source footage.

## Visual grammar

- Alternate luxury-car plates entering vertically; smoke and scanline texture bridges angle changes.
- Hold wheel detail while a brush mask reveals child/teddy; scale the car away.
- Overlap retreating and advancing child/hall plates with title movement in foreground.
- Drop image into radial tunnel, then resolve textured yellow/white chapter typography with scale/rotation settling.
- Spin marque to rest, dip to dark, then build portrait/date/family/mill layers.
- Carry translucent BANKRUPT type over mill/family and rural imagery; use forward zooms and radial exits.
- Use railway-focused zoom into expanding irregular aperture; brighten landscape as boy and birds resolve.
- Darken intimate family detail while school/work imagery replaces it.
- Intercut debt title and food close-ups with HARD LABOUR over worker imagery.
- Shift work/railway into fee close-up, then worker/tools/model-wheelbarrow inserts with radial bridges.
